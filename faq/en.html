Pakt help and frequently asked questions

Pakt is the offline travel translator from Lingofonex. Start with the essentials, then find setup and technical help further down.

About Pakt

What to expect before you download, travel, or go offline.

Pakt is Lingofonex's offline translator for travel. Speak or type short phrases, translate downloaded languages on your phone, and play supported translations aloud. Explore Pakt.

Setup and support

Practical answers for downloads, storage, and technical problems.

Choose the input and output languages, tap the input language button, and speak immediately. You can also type. Short, direct sentences usually produce the clearest result. You can also watch the short Pakt demo.
Offline language models can be large. WiFi usually makes the first download faster and avoids using a substantial amount of mobile data.
Pakt stores language models on your phone so translation can work offline. The download display can occasionally lag behind the completed transfer. If Pakt has trouble starting after a model download, reinstall the latest version and download the required languages again.
Close other apps, check that your phone has enough free storage, and reopen Pakt. If the problem continues, remove Pakt, install the latest version, and download the required language models again.
Use the Lingofonex contact form. Include your device model, software version, and a short description of what happened so we can help faster.
