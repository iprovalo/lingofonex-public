Pakt private translation questions

What makes Pakt a private translation app?

Pakt translates downloaded languages locally on your phone. Offline translation does not require a cloud translation request for each phrase.

Does Pakt need the cloud for offline translation?

No. Once the languages are downloaded, Pakt can translate them on your phone without sending each phrase to a cloud translation service.

Does private translation work without internet?

Yes. Pakt translates downloaded languages on your phone without WiFi or mobile data.

What stays on my phone?

Translation for downloaded languages stays on your phone. Language downloads and app updates still need a connection.

How many languages does Pakt support?

Pakt translates in 100 languages and provides voice output in 50 supported languages.

When is private translation useful while traveling?

Private translation matters when conversations include hotel details, addresses, prices, personal information, health needs, or anything you would rather keep on your phone.
