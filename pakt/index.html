Pakt, in short

What is Pakt?

Pakt is an offline translator for iPhone, made for travel. Speak or type a phrase, see the translation, and play supported results aloud.

Does Pakt work without internet?

Yes. Download the languages you need before the trip, and Pakt can translate those prepared languages when WiFi and mobile data are unavailable.

Can Pakt speak translations aloud?

Yes. Pakt offers voice output in 50 languages. Voice availability depends on the selected language.

How private is Pakt offline translation?

For downloaded languages, translation runs locally on your phone without sending each phrase to a cloud translation service.

How many languages does Pakt support?

Pakt supports translation in 100 languages and voice output in 50. Translation and voice availability are not identical for every language.
