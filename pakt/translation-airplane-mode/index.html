Pakt in airplane mode

Does Pakt work in airplane mode?

Yes. Download your languages before the trip, then Pakt can translate them on your phone without a network connection.

What do I need to download before using Pakt offline?

Download the language you use and the language spoken at your destination before the trip.

Can Pakt translate without WiFi or mobile data?

Yes. Pakt translates downloaded languages on your phone without WiFi or mobile data.

Can Pakt speak translations in airplane mode?

Yes, in 50 supported languages. Pakt translates in 100 languages, so voice output is not available for every language.

How do I test Pakt before a trip?

Turn on airplane mode and try a phrase in both directions before you leave.

Does Pakt send offline translations to the cloud?

No. Once the languages are downloaded, Pakt can translate them on your phone without sending each phrase to a cloud translation service.
